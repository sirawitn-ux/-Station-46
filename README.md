
# 🌙 CLOSING TIME
### First-Person Psychological Horror Game

> *Every night shift has a story. Some are better left untold.*

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Description

**Closing Time** is a 3D first-person psychological horror game where players take on the role of a young man working the night shift at a gas station convenience store.

Players must complete daily tasks assigned by the manager, including restocking shelves, organizing products, and serving customers. However, as the night progresses, strange customers and unexplained events begin to create an increasingly unsettling atmosphere.

As the mystery surrounding a series of murders unfolds, players must stay alert, observe their surroundings, complete their assigned tasks, and find ways to survive the night.

**What begins as an ordinary night shift gradually becomes a terrifying fight for survival.**

---

## 🎯 Game Objectives

### 01. 🕹️ Horror Game Development
Develop a 3D horror game that combines job simulation mechanics with survival gameplay.

### 02. 🌫️ Immersive Atmosphere
Create a tense and frightening atmosphere through environmental design, sound effects, lighting, and unexpected events.

### 03. 👁️ Observation & Decision-Making
Encourage players to observe their surroundings, make critical decisions, and solve problems under pressure.

### 04. 🛠️ Technical Development
Gain practical experience using the Godot Engine to develop 3D environments, character interactions, mission systems, and sound design.

### 05. 💀 Engaging Player Experience
Deliver an exciting and suspenseful experience that keeps players immersed in the story and eager to discover what happens next.

---

## 🕯️ Game Features

- **First-Person Perspective** — Experience the horror through the protagonist's eyes.
- **Night Shift Simulation** — Complete tasks and serve customers at the gas station.
- **Mysterious NPCs** — Encounter strange customers with suspicious behavior.
- **Psychological Horror** — Discover unsettling events as the night progresses.
- **Survival & Suspense** — Stay alert and survive the mysterious killer.
